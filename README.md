Defined classes:

<img width="767" height="731" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7015a041-bbf4-4163-95aa-be4fdae3b708" />

a) Light signals at railway crossings

b) Standard traffic lights for regulating road traffic

c) Variable circular traffic signs

d) Variable triangular traffic signs

e) Green arrow indicating that passage or driving in the lane is permitted

f) Red X indicating that passage or driving in the lane is prohibited

g) Yellow arrow indicating the need to merge into another lane

h) Illuminated text panels with variable content

Models were trained and evaluated on a custom dataset (Results are included in this repository).
The solution and the dataset are part of the master's thesis "Detection of Different Types of Traffic Light Signalization" ("Detekcija različitih tipova svjetlosne prometne signalizacije"), which was completed in October, 2026 at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Computer Science and Information Technology Osijek, under the supervision of prof. dr. sc. Mario Vranješ, prof. dr. sc. Ratko Grbić, and prof. dr. sc. Marijan Herceg.
